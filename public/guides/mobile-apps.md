# Mobile apps

[Human page](/portfolio/mobile-apps) · [Portfolio](/portfolio)

Cross-platform application projects built with React Native and Expo. Open a project on the human page for its current screenshots, features, and technology notes.

- [Time2Pay](/portfolio/mobile-apps/time2pay): time tracking, milestones, and invoices in a self-hostable app, also published at time2pay.app.
- [Medimagen](/portfolio/mobile-apps/medimagen): scan-derived anatomy, CT/MRI alignment, and AR exploration; a Ctrl+Space finalist.

Medimagen, [Neo Rock ’Em Sock ’Em Robots](/portfolio/game-design/neo-rock-em-sock-em-robots), and [Guess the Qubit](/portfolio/game-design/guess-the-qubit) originated in a shared AR prototyping workspace, with shared prototype and TestFlight history. Each experience has its own individual case study; the robot-boxing game and Guess the Qubit are cataloged under Game Design. The workspace itself is not a separate portfolio app.

For deployed software, also see [production apps](/public-facing/production).
